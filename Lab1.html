Hiking/Horseback Riding Map of Boulder

This is an interactive map which displays information about hiking, biking and horseback trails in the Boulder area. The map can be used to find different trail locations in the area.

Hiking Trails

This map shows the various trails for hiking in Boulder County, most located on the western outskirts of the city. They are denoted with a hatched brown line

Trail Heads

Locate the purple trailhead symbols in order to navigate to the trailheads in the area
